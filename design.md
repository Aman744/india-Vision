## Visual Aesthetics
The design language embodies **Modern Retail Premium**. It pairs the visual clarity of Apple and Stripe with the high-tech, reliable presence of Samsung and LG. Key characteristics:
- **Card-Shine Overlay**: Glassmorphic active depth containers with translucent borders and soft, multi-layered shadows.
- **Gradients**: Subtle gradients blending primary (#0056D2) and secondary (#00AEEF) colors to guide user focus.
- **Vibrant Accent**: High-contrast accent orange (#FF6B00) highlights for sales badges, promo codes, and primary calls-to-action.
- **Whitespace**: Spacious padding using the 8px system to prevent cluttered layouts and enhance scannability.
